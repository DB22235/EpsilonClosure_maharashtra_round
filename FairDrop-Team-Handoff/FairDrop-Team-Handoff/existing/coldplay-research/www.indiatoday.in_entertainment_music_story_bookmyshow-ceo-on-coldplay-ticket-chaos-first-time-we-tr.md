BookMyShow CEO on Coldplay ticket chaos: First time we tried democratic ticketing
Ashish Hemrajani, CEO of BookMyShow, addressed the chaos surrounding Coldplay concert tickets in India, explaining that they employed a democratic ticketing system, which required everyone to join a queue and prevented individuals from using their privilege to gain unfair access.

BookMyShow CEO shed light on Coldplay's concert and their ticketing system. (Photo: India Today, Reuters)
By :India Today Entertainment Desk
New Delhi,Jan 29, 2025 00:01 IST

BookMyShow CEO Ashish Hemrajani admitted in an exclusive conversation with India Today's News Director, Rahul Kanwal, how they were underprepared for the overwhelming demand during ticket sales for Coldplay’s concerts, as their platform faced bots. He also talked about how they implemented a democratic ticketing system, so that the privileged people also had to enter the queue to secure their tickets.
