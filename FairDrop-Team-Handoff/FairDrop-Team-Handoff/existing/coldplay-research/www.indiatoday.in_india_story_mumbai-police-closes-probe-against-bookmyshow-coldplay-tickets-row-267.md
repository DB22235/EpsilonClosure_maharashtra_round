Mumbai cops drop case against BookMyShow in Coldplay tickets row: No proof found
The Mumbai Police has closed its preliminary enquiry against BookMyShow for allegedly using ticket bots to manipulate the ticketing process of Coldplay shows in India, saying no proof was found.

Coldplay, the British rock band, visited India after eight years, and performed in Mumbai and Ahmedabad as part of its 'Music of the Spheres' World Tour.
By :Dipesh D. Tripathi
Mumbai,Feb 6, 2025 20:25 IST

The Mumbai Police has closed its preliminary enquiry against online ticketing major BookMyShow for allegedly using ticket bots to manipulate the ticketing process of the recently-concluded Coldplay concerts in India. The case was closed after no evidence of manipulation was found during the investigation.

The Coldplay tickets went on sale via BookMyShow on September 22, 2024, at noon, and got sold out within just 30 minutes, claimed Vyas's plea. Many users reported being logged out of the platform before the sale began, while tickets quickly appeared at prices 30 to 40 times higher than the original.
